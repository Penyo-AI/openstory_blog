## Start with an Accepted Source Clip

Upscaling is a finishing step. First approve the story action, character identity and camera movement. [SeedVR 2 on Plot Party](https://plotparty.ai/seedvr/2) provides an online entry point, so you can work in the browser rather than configuring a local GPU environment. Check the live tool for current input, duration and output limits.

## Prepare Your 480p Source

Use the highest-quality original export available. Avoid a clip downloaded from a social feed if you still have the original. Confirm the aspect ratio and frame rate, and choose a short representative segment containing a face, camera movement and fine texture.

## Upscale and Compare

1. Open the SeedVR 2 page and select the available upscaling workflow.
2. Upload the source clip. Check the preview for the correct crop and orientation.
3. Select the intended output size if supported for that input. For a 4K delivery, confirm the actual dimensions offered by the tool.
4. Review the estimated spend before generating.
5. Download the output and compare it against the source at equal playback size.

| Inspection area | Look for | If it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Faces | Stable eyes, teeth and skin texture | Compare another source or settings |
| Fine detail | Hair and fabric that stay coherent | Avoid aggressive processing |
| Motion | Flickering edges or crawling texture | Review the moving segment again |
| Framing | Correct crop and aspect ratio | Fix the input or output selection |
| Delivery | Actual dimensions, frame rate and format | Confirm export settings |

## Know What Upscaling Cannot Repair

An upscaler can infer detail, but it cannot recover a face that was never rendered correctly or repair a mistaken story action reliably. Regenerate a defective shot in [Seedance 2.5](https://plotparty.ai/seedance/2.5) or [MiniMax H3](https://plotparty.ai/minimax/h3) before spending on a larger export. Keep both source and final files for comparison.

## FAQ

### Does 4K guarantee a better result?

No. Pixel dimensions describe the output size. Judge temporal stability and visible detail separately, especially around eyes, hands and small text.
