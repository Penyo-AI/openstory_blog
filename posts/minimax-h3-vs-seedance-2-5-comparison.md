## Choose by the Shot You Need

A dialogue close-up and a long moving shot put different pressures on a video model. Compare [MiniMax H3](https://plotparty.ai/minimax/h3) and [Seedance 2.5](https://plotparty.ai/seedance/2.5) on the actual scene you plan to ship. This guide provides a repeatable comparison workflow; it does not report a measured head-to-head benchmark.

## Prepare One Shared Brief

Use the same character image, location, action and delivery format. Write one neutral prompt: “A detective enters a dim office, sets an envelope on the desk and turns toward the window. Slow lateral camera movement; preserve the reference outfit.” If you test dialogue, keep the words and language identical.

| Evaluation | What to hold constant | What to record |
|---|---|---|
| Character identity | Reference image and outfit | Face drift across the shot |
| Motion | Action and camera instruction | Contact, gait and camera stability |
| Dialogue | Script and language | Mouth timing and speech clarity |
| Composition | Framing and aspect ratio | Subject placement and crop |
| Production cost | Acceptance criteria | Credits spent per usable shot |

## Run a Small, Fair Trial

1. Check the current duration, audio and reference options on each model page.
2. Generate three attempts per model with the same brief.
3. Review at normal playback speed and frame by frame around transitions.
4. Count usable shots before comparing total spend. A cheap failed attempt still costs time.
5. Repeat with a second scene before committing a whole episode to one engine.

## Make the Production Decision

Prefer the engine that meets your shot requirements with fewer repairs. You can also assign different engines to different scene types, keeping one approved reference set for continuity. For the broader selection process, read the [AI video models guide](./best-ai-video-models-2026-creator-guide.md); for longer-take planning, use the [Seedance 2.5 guide](./seedance-2-5-ai-video-guide.md).

## FAQ

### Is there one winner for every scene?

No measured universal winner is established here. Test your references, action and delivery requirements, then choose by acceptance rate and total production cost.
